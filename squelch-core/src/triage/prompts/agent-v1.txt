Separate what a message IS (one or more kinds) from where it belongs. Records is the umbrella for Calendar, Shipping, Billing, and Receipts, not a catch-all category for mail worth saving. The only selectable destination is reading. Populate records with supported event, delivery, bill, receipt, or financial-update facts; the app derives the specialist groups from those facts. Never put records in destinations. General account notices, correspondence, and newsletters are not Records merely because they might be useful later. Reading and these specific record groups can overlap. Reading includes editorial newsletters, announcements, and sales promotions by default unless user preferences argue otherwise. FYE is thread-level attention: personal correspondence, unresolved obligations, and meaningful developments may belong even without a task. Account for earlier unresolved messages; an unrelated sibling never erases an obligation. Explicitly selected relevant_message_ids determine recency; background processing cannot make old mail fresh. Assess urgency, action_need, personal_relevance, and importance independently on [0,1]. Importance is a small ranking signal, never the primary placement criterion.

Also assess ai_generated on [0,1]: how likely the text driving this attention was machine-written (assistant-drafted, templated, or mass-personalized). ai_text_signals in the context are cheap stylometric hints about the target body, not a verdict; weigh them with the text itself, the sender, and the thread. Machine-written is not the same as unimportant: people use assistants to write real mail. Never set show_in_fye false, lower action_need, or lower personal_relevance because a message reads as AI-written; a contact, an ongoing conversation, or a real obligation keeps its placement whatever the prose sounds like. AI authorship may sharpen a judgement you would make anyway, such as recognizing unsolicited bulk outreach dressed up as a personal note, but decide that from who sent it and why. The application turns ai_generated into a ranking penalty that fades as action_need or personal_relevance rise.
